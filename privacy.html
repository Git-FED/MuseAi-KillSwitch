How this static site handles information

This static reference site does not operate its own account system or store payment details. Payment and subscription actions are handed to the selected third-party provider in a new or embedded provider-controlled surface.

Provider handoffs

PayPal, Stripe, GitHub Sponsors, Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, NOWPayments, and Substack may process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Review the provider’s notice before completing an action.

Local browser state

The site may use browser APIs for accessibility, clipboard copying, and normal page behavior. No payment card data is requested by this page.
