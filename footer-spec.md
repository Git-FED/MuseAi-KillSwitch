# FED-OS Footer — Visual Specification

A restrained, eye-catching footer for the companion site: warm paper, ink, hairlines, one burnt-orange accent, and a printer's registration mark.

## Bands

1. **Brand band:** `FED-OS` / `The Federal Operating System` on the left; `Join Membership →` as an underlined text CTA on the right.
2. **Link grid:** five columns — Ecosystem, Media, Social, Support, Contact — divided by vertical hairlines.
3. **Legal strip:** copyright on the left, Privacy · Terms · Accessibility in the center, Back to top ↑ on the right.

## Signature move

An 8×8 accent square floats just above the 2px top rule at the page margin, like a registration mark. It is the single visual flourish; do not combine it with glow, gradients, pills, or oversized cards.

## Responsive behavior

Collapse from five columns to three at 900px, two at 640px, and one at 440px. Keep all links in normal document order and preserve visible keyboard focus.
